Season ops

Picks stay editable to kickoff; the grading line freezes Tuesday

Five checkpoints turn that flexibility into the same routine every week.

  • Tue -- the pool's line locks at noon and this card's grading line freezes with it.
  • Wed -- the pool revises its own line once; our grading line never moves.
  • Thu -- refresh pass before Thursday night, on what changed since Tuesday.
  • Sat -- second pass for every game that has not kicked off.
  • Sun AM -- final pass before 4:00 PM ET -- Sunday- and Monday-night games lock there too.

Week 1, 2026 locks Tuesday, September 8, 2026.

If the market moves a full point, we follow it. At each pass, if the pool's own line has moved at least 1.0 point off Tuesday's frozen number, the pick follows the market; below that threshold (or with no fresh line captured), the model's own re-run pick plays as always.

Where these numbers come from — the full ladder, the union’s selection caveat, and the out-of-sample re-check

The honest ladder

Played card 54.2% vs a coin flip's 50% -- how good is that, really?

One ladder from guessing to the measured limit this sport allows.

Coin flip: 50%. Raw model before policy overlays: 53.4% at the opener (1,537 games, 2020-2025); 52.1% at the sharper close.

Played chain (raw → coach fade → arrests): 54.2% measured on 1,503 paired games — the measured history under the crowned expectation.

Four-member union: paired +1.26 points on reused data (P+ 0.857); its 55.4% archive score is selection-inflated (best of 127 subsets), and the out-of-sample re-check of that selection measured 0.00 pts (P+ 0.49) — already discounted in the ≈55% expectation.

Movement rule (market-follow on >=1pt moves via refresh): composed +1.53 points (P+ 0.89/0.93) — an attribution upper bound on already-looked-at data.

Best documented long-run bettors: roughly 55-56% against the close. Measured pregame ceiling: about 56% (total-leak control, docs/leak_ceiling_control.md); the older 57-58% band was the pre-measurement guess.

A small step above a coin flip is not proof of a stable, profitable edge — sportsbook vig alone would likely erase it.
