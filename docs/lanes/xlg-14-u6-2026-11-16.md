## Declaration (2026-11-16 15:43 EST, before the script was written or any outcome joined)
- Family `xlg14_schedule_strength_family_u6`. Mechanism: the opener anchors on a team's raw results to date and under-corrects for who those results came against. A team whose production or margin came against strong opponents is better than its raw numbers and under-priced next week; one that padded them against weak opponents is over-priced. Category market. The same construction exists in NFL play-by-play (`epa`, scores, `posteam`/`defteam`).
- Novelty (**read**, grep of registry/weak_signals and ROADMAP for strength of schedule, opponent-adjusted, sos, red zone): MOD-23 unit 3 swapped 36 opponent-adjusted columns into the NFL served ridge as inputs (model alone, -1.46 pts, unresolved); it never tested the adjustment itself (adjusted minus raw) as one add-one term at the opener, and never in college. Red-zone and third-down reversion are spent in the NFL (redzone_reversion*, redzone_third_down_over_fade*). XLG-14 units 1-5 tested workload, schedule rest, luck, scoreboard vs EPA, big-play/leverage composition; none uses opponent quality.
- Inputs: college regular-season scrimmage plays (unit 5 filter: rush or pass, not kneel, EPA present); per team-game offensive and allowed EPA sums and play counts, points (unit 4: max post-play score columns), opponent id. For team t at its game in week w: G = t's games in weeks < w; each opponent's strength is computed from that opponent's games in weeks < w (all of them); league rate L = pooled EPA/play over all team-games of the season in weeks < w. No fitted constant, no cut; each earlier game weighted equally.
- Candidates (0 with no earlier game; home minus away): (1) `off_sos_gap` = minus mean over G of (opponent allowed EPA/play - L): positive = offence faced stingy defences; hypothesis POSITIVE. (2) `def_sos_gap` = mean over G of (opponent offensive EPA/play - L): positive = defence faced strong offences; hypothesis POSITIVE. (3) `margin_sos_gap` = mean over G of opponent points margin per game to date; hypothesis POSITIVE. (4) `last_opponent_strength` = the most recent earlier game's opponent margin per game to date; hypothesis POSITIVE (the last result read raw). Rule two-sided.
