## Declaration (written 2026-11-16 15:49:24 -05:00 = 2026-11-16T20:49:24Z, before freeze and before any Week 11 row)
- Why no fitted coefficient: the 2026 Weeks 2-9 study reads (`artifacts/lead108_wording_mixture/games.parquet`) are one backfill read per player, so no read dispersion exists in the fit window; only Weeks 9-10 have two forward reads (Thursday, Sunday), and Week 10 is excluded. A dispersion slope cannot be fitted out of sample, so the form is fixed with no free constant.
- Term per game: x108 = the LEAD-108 term exactly as `scripts/lead108_forward.py` builds it (latest admissible read per player, LEAD-110 values, clip 1/(reads+2), home minus away). Per admissible read file f, x_f = the same term built from that file's admissible rows only (clip 1/(rows kept in f + 2)), for files with at least one read on the game. s2 = sample variance (ddof 1) of x_f over those files; s2 = 0 with one file. Precision weight w = tau2/(tau2 + s2), the reliability (inverse-variance shrinkage) form, i.e. the ROADMAP "term / (1 + disagreement)" with disagreement = s2/tau2. tau2 = between-game variance (ddof 1) of `x_read` over the 116 study games of Weeks 2-9 (a variance of the input, no outcome used). Term = w x108.
- Coefficient: b = LEAD-108 frozen 0.023836528964368872 logit per point (`artifacts/lead108_forward/frozen.json`, sha recorded at freeze). Delta logit = b w x108. Week 10 excluded; no Week 10+ outcome used.
- Base: last published card home probability at or before run time (LEAD-108 rule). Graded on played card (forward_ledger `_played_card`) at the Tuesday opener, pushes dropped.
- Metric: changed-pick record (term side vs card side, decisive games) first; mean log loss gain vs card with week-stratified bootstrap P+; Brier; log loss gain vs the unweighted LEAD-108 term on the same games (the dispersion contrast); intercept-only control.
