# SIM-07 power attack (2026-11-03)

## Goal
Raise detection power for the personnel x defence-package EPA interaction (18 cells, 326k plays, 2016-2025) by design. Relevant size ~0.005 EPA/play; old instrument (split-half r over 18 cells) needed 0.045 for power .47.

## State
- Cause (inferred from read code): cell SE ~0.011 comes from play-level EPA noise (sd ~1.5) over ~18k plays per cell, and the split-half r design spends half the data per side and judges 18 points. Participation data is already used for every season on disk (2016-2025), so no extra seasons exist.
- New script scripts/sim07_power.py: full-data omnibus Wald statistic over all 18 cells, game-cluster bootstrap null (2000 draws), planted control (6 of 18 cells, random sign, 4000 replicates per size), three labels: raw EPA, fumble-loss-stripped EPA plus pre-snap xpass and wp covariates, success rate.
- Output artifacts/sim07/power_summary.json, log artifacts/sim07/power.log.

## Tried
- Full-data omnibus (rank 10 interaction space, main effects projected out; first draft without projection was miscalibrated, discarded). Runtime 12 s per all three labels.
- Detectable at power 0.8: raw EPA 0.044, fumble-stripped EPA + xpass + wp 0.043, success rate 0.016 (all ~0.033 label sd). Old split-half instrument: 0.045 at power 0.47. Label tweaks buy ~2%; relevant 0.005 has power 0.06 (needs ~77x plays).
- Recorded sim07_personnel_interaction_omnibus_fulldata, unresolved_below_power, no closing ground.

## Next
