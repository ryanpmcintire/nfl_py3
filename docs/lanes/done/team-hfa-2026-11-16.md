## Declaration (stamped 2026-11-16T23:17:56Z, before any outcome joined)
- Family team_hfa_2026_11_16. History REG 2009 onward, data/raw/<latest>/schedules.parquet. Primary venue of team T in season s = the stadium_id hosting most of T's home games that season; games at any other stadium or location Neutral are neutral (term 0, excluded from history).
- Game residual r_g = (result - spread_line) minus that season's league mean over non-neutral REG games (removes the uniform, era-drifting home edge). For team T at venue V and season s: H = mean r_g over T's home games at V in seasons < s; R = mean of -r_g over T's road games in those same seasons (seasons < s with primary venue V); d = H - R (team quality cancels); n_eff = 1/(1/n_H + 1/n_R).
- Shrinkage w = n_eff/(n_eff + lambda), lambda = mean(n_eff) * k/(1-k), k in [0,1) chosen per scored season by inner LOSO: minimise squared error of r_g on w*d over home games of the other screen seasons (bounded scalar search, no grid). Term x = w*d for the home team at its primary venue, 0 otherwise or when no history.
- Declared sign POSITIVE on home cover. ONE term, ONE population (all REG games 2011-2025, pushes dropped), against the close.
- Screen (look 1, gating): OLS slope of home cover on the standardised term, accuracy points per SD; null = 200 within-season shuffles of team labels (each home team takes another team's season value); 2000 season-block bootstraps for interval and probability_positive.
