# Sunday Best Pick matched ranking replay

This one declared comparison ranks the same Sunday 12:45 p.m. ET eligible regular-season games by (1) the frozen Sunday-inclusive heldout probability of the current served side and (2) the archived alpha=2000 probability of that **same** side. Both nominees retain the current served side and opener grade; there is no spread or dispersion screen, new fit, threshold, or live change. The tie breaker is game ID. This plan was saved in the lane before scoring.

Inputs: `artifacts/sunday_market_probability/20260920_fixed/predictions.parquet`, `artifacts/opener_evaluation/20260920T135435Z/per_game.parquet`, `artifacts/ridge_alpha_promotion/20260818T221459Z/opener_paired.parquet`, and `data/processed/game_features.parquet`. The Sunday training replay had dropped 34 eventual pushes before fitting, so its frozen output alone was an invalid operational nominee pool. The corrected replay reconstructs those 34 **candidate** probabilities from the same pregame discrete opener estimates, historical composition flags, Sunday quote archive, and frozen fold coefficients, with no refit. All 1,537 opener games are in the nominee pool. Reconstructed nonpush flag and prediction parity is asserted at runtime. Of 34 pushes, 17 in 2023–25 had Sunday movement archive coverage; the earlier 17 precede that feature's availability era. The old 1,503-game replay is preserved at `artifacts/confidence_best_pick_sunday_matched/20260920_nonpush_conditional/` as an outcome-conditioned diagnostic, not an operational ranking result.

The archived `candidate_prob_open` is home-cover probability, verified in `git show 9f84d09:scripts/ridge_alpha_promotion_eval.py`. Its producer trained each weekly fit only on games completed before that week. Alpha=2000 had already been selected in earlier work, and the NFL population has been reused. There are no alpha LOSO predictions, so the matched comparison uses chronological Sunday predictions only; Sunday's LOSO output is reported separately.
